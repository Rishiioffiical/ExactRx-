ExactRx is designed as a verification and understanding layer, not a replacement for professional clinical judgement.

What ExactRx can do

  • ✓ Identify and organize medicine information.
  • ✓ Explain established medicine information in plain language.
  • ✓ Flag potential safety concerns for review.
  • ✓ Highlight uncertainty and request human confirmation.
  • ✓ Prepare a summary for pharmacist or doctor review.

What ExactRx should not do

  • × Diagnose a medical condition.
  • × Prescribe or independently change treatment.
  • × Tell someone to start or stop a medicine.
  • × Replace pharmacist or doctor verification.
  • × Ignore uncertainty when medicine identity is unclear.
